// STRATEGY

Short-duration residential credit with active oversight.

CSL-RTL provides senior secured financing against residential transitional and value-add projects. Every loan is originated, underwritten, and managed by the Center Street team — no outsourced underwriting, no third-party servicing.

What we finance

  • Residential transitional and value-add projects
  • First-lien, asset-based structures sized to protect principal
  • Shorter expected hold periods relative to traditional private credit
  • Projects where active oversight and execution drive outcomes

Why it exists

Many residential transitional opportunities sit outside the focus of large institutional lenders and traditional private credit funds. CSL-RTL was built to underwrite these assets with the same rigor applied across the firm — conservative structuring, stress-tested downside scenarios, and ongoing portfolio monitoring.

// RISK / RETURN PROFILE

Higher-yielding transitional real estate with active project oversight.

Returns are driven by successful execution and asset-level value creation. The strategy prioritizes principal protection through conservative loan sizing, first-lien security, and continuous monitoring — not yield maximization at the expense of structure.

01 — Sourcing

Proprietary Deal Flow

Origination built on direct relationships, not brokered pipelines. Opportunities are identified and screened by the same team that will underwrite and manage them.

02 — Underwriting

Conservative by Design

Structures sized and collateralized to protect principal first. Downside scenarios are stress-tested before terms are set. Credit Committee oversight on every commitment.

03 — Portfolio Management

Active, Not Passive

Positions monitored on a defined cadence for the life of the investment. Active project oversight where execution risk is material to outcomes.
