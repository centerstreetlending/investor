// APPROACH

A disciplined path from sourcing to outcome.

Every opportunity moves through a repeatable process. The searcher who brings the deal stays accountable to the result.

01 — Source

Proprietary Pipeline

Proprietary, off-market pipeline built through a network of MBA/JD searchers — senior operators, not junior deal-screeners.

02 — Acquire

Underwrite & Close

Rigorous underwriting and diligence against defined investment criteria, with the incoming operator embedded from day one. Center Street infrastructure supports every commitment.

03 — Operate

Aligned Ownership

In equity: the searcher becomes the operating partner running the business — full alignment between who decides and who's accountable. In credit: operator insight informs structure and monitoring.

04 — Grow & Exit

Disciplined Outcome

Active portfolio support toward a disciplined exit, returning capital while preserving what made the business or credit opportunity worth underwriting.
