// THE FIRM

Why Center Street Capital exists.

For more than two decades, Center Street Capital has underwritten credit and businesses the way operators and long-term capital partners actually think — through cycles, with principal protection first, and with real skin in the game. The firm has underwritten more than $7 billion in loans and invested more than $100 million in lower-middle-market private equity, with approximately 90 professionals supporting origination, underwriting, portfolio management, and investor reporting across the platform.

Today the firm manages three complementary strategies: residential value-add lending (CSL-RTL), lower-middle-market direct corporate lending, and operator-led private equity through Riviera Capital. What unifies them is a single underwriting standard, shared governance, and GP capital in every position — we do not outsource underwriting or servicing.

// PHILOSOPHY

The founding premise across every
strategy.

01

Underwriting through cycles

Careers spent underwriting credit and businesses through cycles most managers have only read about — finding return where others don't look, and structuring for the downside before underwriting the upside.

02

Full alignment, not optics

GP capital sits alongside every investor in every position. We do not delegate underwriting or servicing. The team that commits capital remains accountable for the life of the investment.

Capital preservation first

Underwriting discipline and conservative structuring are prioritized over yield maximization. Structure, collateral, and covenants are designed to protect principal across every strategy we bring to market.

// GOVERNANCE

Investment and credit discipline.

Every investment is evaluated through a structured process designed to protect capital at each stage of the lifecycle.

Investment Committee

Investment decisions require approval from the firm’s Investment Committee before capital is committed, across every strategy. The committee brings together credit, equity, and operational perspectives so that structure and downside are tested before capital is deployed.

Credit Committee

A dedicated Credit Committee sets underwriting standards and oversees portfolio monitoring across every position, in every fund. Standards are applied consistently whether the opportunity is residential transitional lending or lower-middle-market corporate credit.

Full committee composition, approval workflows, and portfolio monitoring detail are available to accredited and qualified investors through diligence materials.
