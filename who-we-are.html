// THE FIRM

Why Center Street Capital exists.

For more than two decades, Center Street Capital has underwritten credit and businesses the way operators and long-term capital partners actually think — through cycles, with principal protection first, and with real skin in the game.

The firm has underwritten more than $7 billion in loans and invested more than $100 million in lower-middle-market private equity. Approximately 90 professionals support origination, underwriting, portfolio management, and investor reporting across the platform.

Today the firm manages complementary strategies across private credit and private equity: residential value-add lending (CSL-RTL), lower-middle-market direct corporate lending, and operator-led private equity through Riviera Capital. What unifies them is a single underwriting standard, shared governance, and GP capital in every position.

// PHILOSOPHY

The founding premise across every
strategy.

01

Underwriting through cycles

Careers spent underwriting credit and businesses through cycles most managers have only read about — finding return where others don't look, and structuring for the downside before underwriting the upside.

02

Full alignment, not optics

GP capital sits alongside every investor in every position. We do not delegate underwriting or servicing. The team that commits capital remains accountable for the life of the investment.

03

Capital preservation first

Underwriting discipline and conservative structuring are prioritized over yield maximization. Structure, collateral, and covenants are designed to protect principal across every strategy we bring to market.
