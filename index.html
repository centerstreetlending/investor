// WHO WE SERVE

Built for sophisticated capital partners.

We work with institutional investors, individual investors, and financial intermediaries. Each strategy
is designed for a distinct role in an allocation.

Institutional Investor

  • Pension fund
  • Insurance provider
  • Investment consultant
  • Endowment or foundation
  • Family office (institutional)

Typically seeking differentiated private credit income, lower-middle-market exposure, or operator-aligned private equity with strong governance and reporting.

Individual Investor

  • Private investor / HNW individual
  • Client of an investment advisor
  • Client of a wealth manager or private bank

Often looking for current income, capital preservation features, or access to operator-led private equity difficult to reach through traditional channels.

Financial Intermediary

  • Registered investment advisor (RIA)
  • Independent broker-dealer
  • Wirehouse
  • Wealth manager
  • Private bank

Partners who need institutional-quality private credit and private equity solutions with clear risk/return profiles and robust diligence materials for clients.
